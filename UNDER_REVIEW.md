## Promotion rule

A record may move into models_final.json only after the stated reconsideration condition is met, primary evidence is refreshed, and the full maintenance gate passes.

The machine-readable source is `records_under_review.json`; its contract is `records_under_review.schema.json`. The dated source-review ledgers remain the evidence authority.
